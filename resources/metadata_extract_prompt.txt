OPTIONAL PUBLICATION METADATA EXTRACTION HELPER

This file is an optional prompt for use with an AI outside the app. No AI use,
credentials, invocation record, or completion marker is required by the app.
The app neither displays nor executes this prompt.

TASK AND OUTPUT

Use the supplied scientific paper, supplementary information, and supplied
sample/file annotations to draft Samples.csv for the biological scRNA-seq
samples actually represented in the prepared dataset. The user must review the
draft against the expression files, choose the final imported samples, and fill
n_cells from the final prepared cell tables. Publication metadata is not proof
of expression/cell/gene alignment or successful standardization.

Return the COMPLETE CSV for ALL identified relevant samples in one csv code
block, without commentary inside the CSV. Do not return YAML, a samples: wrapper,
a Markdown table, or only changed rows. Use exactly this header as one record:

sample,technology,cancer_type,patient,histology,site,sample_type,sample_primary_met,disease_extent,diagnosis_recurrence,instance_at_site,treated_naive,age,sex,grade,AJCC_stage,AJCC_T,AJCC_N,AJCC_M,size,smoking_status,PY,KI67,genetic_hormonal_features,chemotherapy_exposed,chemotherapy_response,targeted_rx_exposed,targeted_rx_response,post_sampling_rx_exposed,post_sampling_rx_response,time_end_of_rx_to_sampling,ET_exposed,ET_response,ICB_exposed,ICB_response,PFS_DFS,OS,n_cells

CSV RULES

- Use UTF-8 text and a header followed by one row per biological sample.
  Every record must have the same number of fields as the header.
- Preserve identifiers as text, including leading zeros (001, 0007). Do not
  turn identifiers into numbers or spreadsheet dates.
- Use empty fields for unknown, ambiguous, inapplicable, or deliberately
  unfilled values. Do not use literal NA, null, unknown, or a guessed value.
- Quote fields containing commas, double quotes, or newlines. Within a quoted
  field, double each embedded quote. For example, the field Colon, ascending
  is written "Colon, ascending"; Source calls this "primary" is written
  "Source calls this ""primary""".
- Keep all headers even if an entire column is blank. No extra evidence columns
  are necessary; if explaining uncertainty, put notes outside the CSV.
- The sample field must be non-empty and unique. Prefer author-defined sample
  IDs, with explicit mappings if canonicalization is necessary. IDs must be
  safe single directory names on Windows and Linux: no path separators,
  surrounding whitespace, control characters, trailing periods, . or ..,
  characters < > : " | ? *, reserved device names such as CON/NUL/COM1 (including
  CON.txt), or IDs that differ only by capitalization. Keep IDs consistent with
  sample folders and cell metadata; do not invent unsupported biological samples.
- Leave n_cells blank for EVERY row in this extraction draft, even if the paper
  reports cell counts. The user fills it from final prepared Cells.csv row
  counts after filtering and before import. The app does not calculate it at
  import or certify the reported count.

EVIDENCE AND SAMPLE MEMBERSHIP

1. Be conservative. Use explicit evidence from the paper, supplements, methods,
   sample annotations, and supplied data. Do not fill fields from clinical
   expectations, indirect implications, or information about another patient.
   Leave unresolved conflicts between sources blank and explain them separately.
2. Count biological samples, not patients, sequencing lanes, cell clusters,
   library batches, or technical replicates unless they genuinely represent
   distinct biological specimens. One patient can contribute primary tumor,
   metastasis, and blood as three samples. Use recognizable author-defined IDs;
   if a combined identifier is needed, use a clear patient/site mapping.
3. Distinguish patient-level and specimen-level evidence. Age, sex, disease
   diagnosis, histology, stage, and molecular features may be repeated across
   that patient's samples only if their scope and timing support this.
   Site, lesion size, specimen status, occurrence at that site, and treatment
   timing must not be copied between specimens without evidence.
4. Apply cohort-wide statements only to the population they actually describe.
   "All patients were MSS" and "Patients P03, P05 and P06 received chemotherapy"
   have different scopes. Do not extrapolate between groups.

INTERPRETATION RULES

- site is strictly anatomical: Liver, Ascending colon, Upper rectum, Peripheral
  blood, Left ovary, Axillary lymph node. Exclude biopsy, resection specimen,
  fresh tissue, tumor core, and similar collection/processing descriptions
  unless they identify a meaningful anatomical subsite.
- size is the physical size of the EXACT sampled lesion. Include source units.
  A maximum metastasis diameter, cohort mean, largest lesion, or radiological
  burden cannot be assigned to an unspecified lesion in a multi-lesion patient.
- Treatment exposure describes treatment BEFORE that sample was collected.
  Subsequent treatment belongs in the post-sampling fields. Do not mix
  chemotherapy, targeted treatment, endocrine therapy, immune checkpoint
  blockade, or radiotherapy. Follow the supplied meaning of rx fields; if the
  corresponding modality is ambiguous, leave them blank rather than invent a
  modality. Never put chemotherapy timing in a radiotherapy timing field.
- treated_naive is treated or naive when supported; it does not imply exposure
  to every treatment modality. Populate each modality separately.
- diagnosis_recurrence describes the overall disease episode: diagnosis for
  initial/continuous disease, recurrence after disease returned following a
  complete remission. Persistent or resistant disease without remission is
  not automatically recurrence. Metastatic, advanced, treated, or resected
  disease alone does not establish recurrence.
- instance_at_site is the ordinal occurrence at the SPECIFIC disease site:
  0 for the first occurrence, 1 for the first recurrence there, 2 and higher for
  later recurrences there. It is not stage, metastatic status, lesion count,
  sample count, or treatment count. Do not assume 0 simply because one specimen
  is listed; leave it blank if prior disease at that site cannot be excluded.
  Initial primary tumor with synchronous liver metastasis can have 0 at each
  site. Recurrence at the primary site with a newly appearing liver metastasis
  can have diagnosis_recurrence=recurrence for both, with instance_at_site=1
  at the primary site and 0 at the liver site, when that history is supported.
- disease_extent describes the PATIENT's disease; sample_primary_met describes
  the individual tumor specimen. A primary tumor from a patient with distant
  metastases may have sample_primary_met=primary and disease_extent=metastatic.
  A lymph-node metastasis is still a metastatic specimen.
- Do not force non-tumor samples into tumor-only categories. For blood from a
  solid-tumor patient, sample_type=blood while sample_primary_met and
  instance_at_site are blank if inapplicable. Patient-level cancer information
  can still describe the donor. A solid-tumor metastasis in bone/bone marrow is
  sample_type=tumor, with the anatomical location in site.
- A normal specimen from a cancer patient can have sample_type=normal while
  cancer_type still describes the donor's disease. Do not equate tissue
  histology with the patient's diagnosis.

FIELD REFERENCE

sample: Canonical sample ID; use the author's ID where possible.
technology: Sequencing technology, e.g. 10x Genomics or SmartSeq2; include
  chemistry/platform version only when supported.
cancer_type: Patient's cancer/premalignant disease in full, with standardized
  capitalization. When a supplied cancer_cell_map.json vocabulary is available,
  use its exact matching disease name where evidence supports it. Examples
  include Colorectal Cancer, Breast Cancer, Lung Adenocarcinoma, and Lung
  Squamous Cell Carcinoma. Do not force an unsupported disease into a known
  category just to obtain marker matching; unknown values can be blank.
patient: Patient ID, preserving leading zeros.
histology: Histological subtype, e.g. adenocarcinoma, mucinous, ductal, lobular.
site: Anatomical sampling location only.
sample_type: Specimen category, e.g. tumor, normal, premalignant, lymph node,
  blood, pleural effusion, ascites, bone marrow.
sample_primary_met: primary or metastasis for applicable tumor specimens;
  leave blank when not applicable. Recurrent primary-site tumors remain
  primary; recurrent metastatic-site tumors remain metastasis.
disease_extent: normal, premalignant, primary, or metastatic when supported.
diagnosis_recurrence: diagnosis or recurrence as defined above.
instance_at_site: 0, 1, 2, etc. only when site-specific history supports it.
treated_naive: treated or naive before sampling when supported.
age: Patient age at sampling; preserve units if not years.
sex: Reported patient sex.
grade: Reported tumor grade.
AJCC_stage: Reported AJCC stage.
AJCC_T: Reported T category.
AJCC_N: Reported N category.
AJCC_M: Reported M category.
size: Exact sampled lesion's size, with units.
smoking_status: Reported history, e.g. active, former, never smoker.
PY: Pack-years.
KI67: Reported Ki67 quantification.
genetic_hormonal_features: Supported genetic, molecular, or hormonal features,
  e.g. ER+, TP53-mut, MSS, MSI, pMMR, dMMR.
chemotherapy_exposed: Exposure to chemotherapy before sampling.
chemotherapy_response: Response to that pre-sampling chemotherapy.
targeted_rx_exposed: Exposure before sampling to the modality explicitly
  defined for this field in supplied source/schema documentation.
targeted_rx_response: Response to that corresponding pre-sampling treatment.
post_sampling_rx_exposed: Corresponding treatment received after sampling.
post_sampling_rx_response: Response to that post-sampling treatment.
time_end_of_rx_to_sampling: Time from the end of the corresponding treatment
  to sampling, with units; do not substitute an unrelated modality.
ET_exposed: Endocrine/hormone therapy exposure before sampling.
ET_response: Response to that pre-sampling endocrine therapy.
ICB_exposed: Immune checkpoint blockade exposure before sampling.
ICB_response: Response to that pre-sampling immune checkpoint blockade.
PFS_DFS: Reported progression-free or disease-free survival; retain which
  endpoint, units, and censoring when provided.
OS: Reported overall survival, with units/censoring when provided.
n_cells: Always blank in this publication-extraction draft. The user must fill
  final prepared cell counts before importing. Never use publication counts.

FINAL CROSS-CHECK

Check patient/sample counts, IDs, tissue/site assignments, primary/metastatic
specimen status, disease extent, treatment timing/modalities, diagnosis versus
recurrence, site-specific occurrence, patient-level versus lesion-level
variables, and exact CSV field counts. Resolve uncertainty conservatively.
Return one complete CSV code block containing the header and every sample row.
Do not omit columns or infer information to make the table look complete.
